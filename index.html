Sacred Heritage

Guru-Shishya Parampara

Kathak is not merely an art form; it is storytelling through devotional grace, mathematical brilliance, and emotive depth. At Kathak Kala Kendra, we preserve this lineage with unconditional devotion.

Lineage & Styles

The Three Great Gharanas

While preserving distinct characteristics, our syllabus provides comprehensive exposure to all classical Kathak styles.

NAWABI ELEGANCE

Lucknow Gharana

Renowned for refined grace, subtle facial expressions (Bhav/Abhinaya), delicacy in Gat Nikas, and poetic interpretation of Thumris and Ghazals.

  • Emphasis on delicate torso bends
  • Kalka-Bindadin lineage tradition
VEER RASA & SPEED

Jaipur Gharana

Celebrated for complex rhythmic structures (Tala), powerful energetic footwork (Tatkar), rapid multi-spin chakkars, and vigorous Kavitts.

  • Pakhawaj-based vigorous bols
  • Expertise in complicated layas
DEVOTIONAL PURITY

Banaras Gharana

Distinctive for its spiritual sanctity, robust floor contact (Utplavana), expansive use of Natwari bols, and devotional Shiva-Parvati Vandanas.

  • Janki Prasad ji tradition
  • Sublime Natwari dance syllables
